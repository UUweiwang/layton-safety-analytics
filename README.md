# Layton Construction | Safety Analytics

**KPMG Layton Data Analytics Challenge 2026**  
University of Utah — Student Analytics Team

*From Safety Data to Preventive Action*

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive construction safety analytics dashboard developed using synthetic competition data.

Our objective is to identify potential safety risks, strengthen preventive practices, and support evidence-based management decisions.

**[View Live Streamlit Dashboard](https://layton-safety-analytics.streamlit.app/)**

## Data & Analytical Approach

The analysis covers 40 construction projects from January 2024 through September 2026, representing approximately 10.19 million working hours.

Our approach combines exposure-adjusted safety metrics, descriptive analysis, project-level comparisons, correlation analysis, and illustrative financial scenarios.

### Key Portfolio Metrics

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Construction Projects | 40 |
| Qualifying Incident Rate | 0.923 per 200,000 hours |
| Daily PTP Compliance | 87.25% |
| Weekly Focus Walk Compliance | 83.15% |

## Interactive Dashboard

The Streamlit application contains seven pages:

1. **Executive Overview** — Portfolio metrics and integrated safety insights.
2. **Workforce Risk** — Overtime, headcount, incident rates, and project-level review.
3. **Hazards Prevention** — Serious hazards, injury severity, and prevention priorities.
4. **Safety Compliance** — Daily PTP and Weekly Focus Walk performance.
5. **Cost-Benefit Simulator** — Adjustable financial assumptions, scenarios, and sensitivity analysis.
6. **90-Day Safety Pilot** — Four-project selection, implementation roadmap, budget, and success measures.
7. **Executive Summary** — Narrated findings and strategic recommendations.

## Management Recommendations

We recommend a targeted 90-day pilot across four projects rather than an immediate company-wide rollout.

The proposed pilot focuses on improving preventive compliance, verifying serious hazard resolution, and strengthening management visibility.

The illustrative pilot budget is **$29,210**. Short-term injury-cost scenarios do not demonstrate easy financial break-even.

**Our recommendation: Start small, measure results, and scale based on verified performance.**

## Limitations & Disclaimer

This academic project uses synthetic competition data, not actual Layton operational records.

Observed correlations do not establish causation. Financial costs, intervention effectiveness, and ROI results are illustrative assumptions, not validated savings forecasts.

This is an independent student project and is not an official product of Layton Construction or KPMG.
